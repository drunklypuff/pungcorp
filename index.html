Privacy Policy

Protein & Calorie Tracker · Effective November 12, 2026

This policy explains how Protein & Calorie Tracker (“the app”), developed by PungCorp (“we”, “us”), handles your information. The short version: your food log stays on your device, we don’t run any servers, and we don’t collect or sell your personal data.

Information stored on your device

The meals, foods, calories, protein and goals you enter are saved locally on your device. This data is not uploaded to us or to any server we operate, and we have no access to it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

Food search (Google Gemini API)

When you use food search, the text you type (for example, “grilled chicken breast 6 oz”) is sent to Google’s Gemini API to look up nutrition information, and the result is returned to the app. The app does not log or store these search requests anywhere other than on your device if you choose to save the result.

Search requests are processed by Google under the paid tier of the Gemini API, governed by the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Under those terms, Google does not use these requests or its responses to improve its products or train its models; it may retain them for a limited period only to detect abuse and comply with legal obligations. The search box only needs the name and amount of a food, so please don’t enter other personal information.

No account, name, email address, device identifier or location is sent with a search.

Purchases

The app is sold through Google Play, and Google processes your payment. We never receive your payment card or billing details. Google provides us with limited order information (such as an order number, purchase date and country) so we can handle sales, refunds and tax requirements. How Google handles purchase data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

The app asks for permission to send notifications, such as meal or logging reminders. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off at any time in your device’s settings.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No advertising, and no ad or tracking SDKs.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.
  • No sale or sharing of your data with anyone for marketing.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you are in full control of it. You can delete individual entries in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Health information

The app is a general nutrition tracker and is not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
