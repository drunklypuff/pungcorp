Frequently asked questions

How do I log a meal?

Tap Add Meal, type a food description (for example “6 oz grilled chicken”), and enter the protein and calories, or let the AI fill them in. Tap Add another food to log several foods at once, then Save.

How do AI estimates work?

Tap the ✨ button next to a food description, or Smart Fill when you have several foods, and the app asks Google’s Gemini AI for protein and calorie estimates. Only the food description you typed is sent; nothing else. The more specific the description (amount and preparation), the better the estimate. AI estimates can be wrong, so check labels when accuracy matters.

An AI lookup failed. What can I do?

  • Check that you’re connected to the internet. AI lookups need a connection; everything else works offline.
  • Each device can make up to 150 AI lookups per day. If you reach the limit, you can still enter values yourself, and lookups reset the next day.
  • If you see “busy”, wait a minute and try again.
  • Try a more specific description, or enter the values yourself.

How do I turn AI lookups on or off?

Open Settings (sliders icon, top right) and use the AI food lookups switch. When it’s off, nothing is sent to Google and you enter values yourself.

How do I change my daily goals?

Open Settings and change your daily protein and calorie goals, then tap Save. You can also switch between light, dark and automatic appearance there.

How do I edit or delete a meal?

Tap the meal in your list to edit it. To delete it, tap the trash icon at the top of the edit screen.

How do I see other days?

Use the arrows or the calendar icon next to the date on the Today screen. The Weekly Report tab shows totals and daily averages for each week.

My meal reminders aren’t appearing.

Check the Reminders tab to make sure reminders are switched on and your times are set. Then make sure notifications are allowed for PC P&C Tracker in your phone’s settings (on iPhone: Settings › Notifications; on Android: Settings › Apps › PC P&C Tracker › Notifications).

Where is my data stored? Can I move it to a new phone?

Your food log and goals are stored only on your device. There’s no account and no cloud sync, so data doesn’t move between devices. Deleting the app deletes its data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is this medical advice?

No. PC P&C Tracker is a general nutrition-tracking tool and its values are estimates. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before making significant changes to your diet.
